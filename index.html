Shorts Pipeline

Shorts Pipeline is a private, server-side tool used by a single creator to manage their own short-form video channels.

It creates short vertical clips (10–50 seconds) from long-form videos, adds text overlays, uploads them privately to the creator's own YouTube channel and, optionally, sends them as drafts to the creator's own TikTok inbox. Publishing on TikTok is always done manually by the creator in the TikTok app.

The tool has no other users and does not access data of anyone other than the creator who operates it.
